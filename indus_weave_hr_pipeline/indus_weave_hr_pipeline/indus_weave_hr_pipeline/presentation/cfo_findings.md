# CFO Findings: Why Overtime Costs Are Rising

1. **Finding 1: Payroll Pays Unverified Overtime Requests**
   - **Evidence:** Query 5 shows paid overtime hours consistently track approved overtime requests rather than actual biometric punch calculations.
   - **Action:** Enforce automated 3-way matching between biometric punch overtime, approved requests, and payroll disbursement.

2. **Finding 2: Unauthorized Overtime Discrepancies in Plant Locations**
   - **Evidence:** Query 6 identifies that Korangi and SITE plants exceed computed overtime by over 15% due to unverified shift extensions.
   - **Action:** Implement mandatory badge-out sign-offs by floor supervisors.

3. **Finding 3: Ghost Payroll Payments After Separation**
   - **Evidence:** Query 8 reveals employees receiving disbursements after their official separation dates.
   - **Action:** Link payroll active status directly to active employment history end-dates.
